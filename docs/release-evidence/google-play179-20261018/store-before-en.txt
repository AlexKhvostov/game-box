									127 text entry area (settable) Полное описание, Value: One little cube. Moving obstacles. How long can you keep them apart?

Untouch is a dodge arcade game where every close call matters. Slide your finger to guide the mint-green cube around the arena, watch the red shapes and find a way through. Stay clear for a little longer, then see if you can beat your own time.

EASY TO START, ROOM TO IMPROVE
Touch and move to take control. Read the gaps, change direction and keep your cool as the obstacles pick up speed. A small movement can save a run; a careless one can end it.

MORE THAN A STOPWATCH
See your survival time, close-call risks and distance travelled. Compare your results with your personal best and explore the leaderboard. Each attempt is another chance to find a better route.

A LITTLE EXTRA HELP
Use crystals to rent Jump or Helmet. Jump lets you leap over an enemy with a second finger; Helmet protects you from one hit per run. These abilities require an active rental.

YOUR NEXT ATTEMPT
Lives are used for attempts, so choose your moments. Follow your progress, adjust music and sound effects, and come back with a new idea for the next run.

Untouch supports English and Russian. Network features, including the shared leaderboard, require an internet connection.
