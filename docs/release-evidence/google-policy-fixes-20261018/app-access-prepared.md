## English instructions for the open guest path

1. Launch **untouch** (Android).
2. Select the appropriate age group. This question does not grant advertising consent.
3. The Google account connection is optional for ordinary gameplay. Choose **Just play** or cancel the native account chooser to continue as a guest.
4. Touch and hold the game field to start; move your finger to move the player. A collision ends the attempt; inspect the result or open rankings from the header.
5. Tap the energy balance to open the Energy window. Tap **Watch** to open the rewarded-ad instructions. The instructions page contains the separate **Watch ad** launch button and the advertising-data explanation. Advertising requires a separate voluntary choice and an eligible age group. The ad network controls its own skip/end-card/close buttons; close the finished ad to return to the game.
6. In rankings tap another player's record, then **Report / hide name**. Public name rules are offered before first publishing an edited name or making a Google name public.
7. The Profile includes help, privacy information, the player ID, Google connection and restoration.
