04 / BEFORE YOUR FIRST TRY

Ready when
you are.

Do I need to download an app?

Get the Android app on Google Play — it’s publicly available, with no test invitation needed. You can also play in Telegram without installing a separate app. The iPhone version is still in development. Try three demo runs right here on the website.

How do I hold my phone and move the cube?

Hold your phone upright. In the full game, drag your finger anywhere on the screen to move the cube. In this demo, drag on the arena, or use the arrow keys on a computer. The edges keep you inside; touching a moving shape ends your run.

Can I compete with friends?

Yes. Compare your best runs and check the leaderboard. Everyone plays their own run, and every new record is a reason to challenge a friend again.

What do the screenshots show?

They show the Android app with sample scores, balances, and results. The screenshots match the selected website language. The Telegram interface may look different.
